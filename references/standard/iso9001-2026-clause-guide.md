# ISO 9001:2026 Clause Guide for Audit Reasoning

This guide summarizes audit intent and evidence expectations. It is not a replacement for the full ISO 9001:2026 PDF. Use it for planning, checklist generation, and first-pass clause mapping; use the PDF for exact wording. The registered PDF (`assets/standards/ISO_9001_2026.pdf`, published Sixth edition, OCR text layer) is the source for exact wording; this guide was first written from FDIS-stage text and a full-text comparison (2026-10-05) found wording differences in 7.3, 7.5.2, 8.1, 8.3.2, 8.5.6, 8.6 and 9.2.2 — see `assets/requirement_profiles/README.md`; for those, use the PDF.

## 2026 audit themes to check
- **Quality culture and ethical behaviour**: appears in leadership, awareness, and environment-for-process thinking. Audit it through leadership actions, communication, behaviours, training/awareness, decision-making, and process environment evidence.
- **Separated risks and opportunities**: risks and opportunities are treated distinctly in planning; do not collapse both into a single risk register unless the organization can show both are determined, analysed, evaluated, acted on, and effectiveness reviewed.
- **Strengthened management of change**: planning of QMS changes now expects consideration of purpose/consequences, QMS integrity, resources/information, responsibilities, communication, monitoring/evaluation, and review of change results.
- **Climate change relevance**: context and interested-party analysis require consideration/monitoring when relevant.
- **Organizational knowledge**: retained, applied, and shared to the extent necessary; changing needs/trends should trigger acquisition/access to additional knowledge.
- **Technology/digital system impacts**: Annex A clarifies impacts on information reliability, responsibilities, communication, infrastructure, knowledge, and monitoring/measurement.

## Clause-by-clause audit lens

### 4 Context of the organization
- **4.1**: Verify method and evidence for determining internal/external issues, strategic direction relevance, climate change relevance, and monitoring/review.
- **4.2**: Verify relevant interested parties, their requirements, which are addressed by the QMS, and monitoring/review.
- **4.3**: Verify scope boundaries, products/services, applicability of requirements, justifications for not-applicable requirements, and controlled documented scope.
- **4.4**: Verify QMS process architecture: inputs/outputs, sequence/interactions, criteria/methods/KPIs, resources, responsibilities, risks/opportunities, evaluation, changes, and process improvement.

### 5 Leadership
- **5.1.1**: Verify visible leadership, integration of QMS into business processes, resources, communication, customer focus, process approach, risk/opportunity thinking, continual improvement, quality culture, ethical behaviour, and accountability.
- **5.1.2**: Verify customer requirements, statutory/regulatory requirements, risks/opportunities affecting conformity and satisfaction, and customer satisfaction focus.
- **5.2**: Verify quality policy suitability, commitments, strategic alignment, documented availability, communication, understanding, application, and availability to interested parties.
- **5.3**: Verify assigned/communicated responsibilities and authorities for QMS conformity, performance reporting, process intended results, customer focus, improvement reporting, and QMS integrity during changes.

### 6 Planning
- **6.1.1**: Verify issues and interested-party requirements feed planning, and risks/opportunities are determined for assurance, prevention/reduction, continual improvement, and enhancement of desired effects.
- **6.1.2**: Verify risks are determined, analysed, evaluated, actions planned, integrated into processes, and effectiveness evaluated; action proportionality must be justified.
- **6.1.3**: Verify opportunities are determined, analysed, evaluated, actions planned, integrated into processes, effectiveness evaluated, and appropriate to context.
- **6.2**: Verify objectives are established at relevant functions/levels/processes, measurable, monitored, communicated, updated, documented, and linked to conformity/satisfaction.
- **6.3**: Verify change planning across purpose, consequences, QMS integrity, resources/information, responsibilities, communication, monitoring/evaluation, and results review.

### 7 Support
- **7.1.1-7.1.3**: Verify resource determination, internal constraints, external provider needs, people, infrastructure, and maintenance.
- **7.1.4**: Verify process environment factors including social, psychological, physical, and quality culture/ethical behaviour influences where relevant.
- **7.1.5**: Verify monitoring and measuring resources, suitability, maintenance, evidence of fitness, calibration/verification/traceability where required, status identification, safeguards, and response to invalid equipment.
- **7.1.6**: Verify organizational knowledge necessary for process operation and QMS intended results; retained, applied, shared, and updated for changing needs/trends.
- **7.2**: Verify competence needs, competence basis, actions to acquire competence, effectiveness evaluation, and evidence.
- **7.3**: Verify awareness of quality policy, contribution, nonconformity implications, relevant objectives, quality culture, and ethical behaviour.
- **7.4**: Verify communication planning: what, when, with whom, how, and who communicates.
- **7.5**: Verify documented information required by the standard and by the organization, creation/update controls, access/use/storage/version/retention/disposition, external-origin controls, and protection of conformity evidence.

### 8 Operation
- **8.1**: Verify operational planning and control, product/service requirements, acceptance criteria, process criteria, controls, resources, evidence, planned/unintended changes, and controls for external provision.
- **8.2**: Verify customer communication, requirement determination, requirement review before commitment, resolution of differences, documented evidence, and communication of changes.
- **8.3**: Verify design and development process, planning, inputs, controls, outputs, and changes. If excluded/not applicable, verify scope justification and no adverse effect on conformity/satisfaction.
- **8.4**: Verify external provider controls, evaluation/selection/monitoring/re-evaluation criteria, type and extent of control, provider communication, verification/validation, and evidence.
- **8.5**: Verify controlled conditions, product/service information, monitoring/measurement, infrastructure/environment, competence, process validation/revalidation, human error prevention, release/delivery/post-delivery, traceability, customer/external provider property, preservation, and production/service change controls.
- **8.6**: Verify release arrangements, acceptance evidence, authority and customer approval where applicable, and traceability to releaser.
- **8.7**: Verify identification/control of nonconforming outputs, actions based on nature/effect, correction/containment/customer notification/concession, re-verification, and records.

### 9 Performance evaluation
- **9.1**: Verify monitoring/measurement needs, methods, timing, analysis/evaluation timing, QMS performance/effectiveness evaluation, customer satisfaction monitoring, and analysis outputs.
- **9.2**: Verify audit programme, frequency/methods/responsibilities/planning/reporting, importance of processes, previous audits, changes, objectives/criteria/scope, impartial auditors, reporting, and timely correction/CAPA.
- **9.3**: Verify management review intervals, suitability/adequacy/effectiveness/strategic alignment, complete inputs, decisions, changes, resource needs, and evidence.

### 10 Improvement
- **10.1**: Verify continual improvement uses monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation, and management review outputs to determine and address opportunities.
- **10.2**: Verify NC reaction, control/correction/consequences, cause analysis, recurrence/occurrence elsewhere, action implementation, effectiveness review, risks/opportunities updates, QMS changes, proportionality, and records.
