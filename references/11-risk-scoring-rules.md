# Risk Scoring Rules

## Purpose
Score audit risk to guide sampling, audit focus, and reviewer attention. Risk scoring does not decide conformity.

## Risk Drivers
Consider:
- NC history and recurrence.
- Recent process, personnel, supplier, equipment, or system changes.
- Process criticality and effect on customer or regulatory requirements.
- Supplier/external provider dependency.
- Competence sensitivity.
- Complexity and variability.
- Monitoring performance trends.
- Structural AHP score of related clauses.

## Scale
| Score | Level | Guidance |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Low | normal sampling |
| 2 | Low-medium | light additional focus |
| 3 | Medium | targeted sampling and evidence review |
| 4 | High | increased sampling and COV emphasis |
| 5 | Very high | deep sampling, senior review, escalation likely |

## Output
Provide risk score, risk level, drivers, sampling recommendation, audit focus, and any review triggers.

## v2 High-Risk Sampling Example: Calibration / Measurement System
When calibration or measurement control has repeated NCs, a customer complaint linked to measurement discrepancy, recent software/database migration, or incomplete competence evidence, consider risk score 4-5.

Sampling should include:
1. Migrated calibration records before and after migration.
2. Equipment status and overdue calibration list.
3. Sample of equipment used for affected product/processes.
4. Access control and change logs for the calibration database.
5. Training and competence evidence for users of the new system.
6. Complaint investigation linkage to measurement validity.
7. Evidence of correction and corrective action for repeated findings.

Recommend technical reviewer involvement when measurement validity, product acceptance, or customer complaint impact requires specialized judgement.


## ISO 9001:2026 Risk and Opportunity Factors

For ISO 9001:2026, score audit risk higher when:

- Risks and opportunities are not separated or are treated as a single generic list.
- Actions to address risks or opportunities lack effectiveness review.
- QMS changes lack consequence review, communication, monitoring/evaluation, or result review.
- Quality culture or ethical behaviour issues can affect product/service conformity or customer confidence.
- Technology, software, infrastructure, remote/hybrid work, or digital records affect QMS integrity.
- Organizational knowledge loss could affect process performance or conformity.

For high-risk calibration/measurement or software migration cases, increase sampling of:

1. Migrated records and data integrity checks
2. Equipment status and overdue calibration
3. Personnel competence for the new system
4. Complaints or product impact linked to measurement results
5. Access control and evidence alteration protection
