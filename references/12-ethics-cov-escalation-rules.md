# Ethics, Chain-of-Verification, and Escalation Rules

## Ethics and Impartiality Screen
Use this screen for high-risk findings, `Noncomplied` verdicts, potential Major NC, sensitive clauses, or conclusions that could materially affect certification.

Check:
- Is the conclusion evidence-based?
- Is there bias, overclaiming, or unsupported severity?
- Is the auditor exceeding competence or authority?
- Are alternatives fairly considered?

## Sensitive Clause Baseline
`7.2, 7.3, 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.7, 9.3, 10.2`

## Chain-of-Verification Questions
Before finalizing material conclusions, ask:
1. Were all applicable requirement elements tested?
2. Are evidence pointers or evidence descriptions sufficiently clear?
3. What evidence is missing or weak?
4. Is there a reasonable alternative interpretation?

## Escalation Triggers
Use `ReviewRequired` or prepare escalation packet when:
- confidence is below 0.80 for material decisions;
- evidence conflicts across sources;
- evidence is missing, stale, or metadata-poor;
- high AHP score combines with partial evidence;
- sensitive clauses are involved;
- potential Major NC exists;
- conclusion depends on expert interpretation.

## Escalation Packet
Include reviewer role, reason for escalation, minimum additional evidence, ambiguity/conflict, and recommended next step.

## v2 Enhanced Sensitive / High-Impact Triggers
Add enhanced COV and consider escalation for:

- `8.6` release of products and services;
- product shipped or service delivered before required verification/acceptance is complete;
- missing release authorization, missing acceptance result, or unsigned final inspection where release occurred;
- corrective action closure for repeated, customer-impacting, or sensitive issues;
- requests to conclude certification readiness or certification decision from summary evidence.

Updated sensitive clause baseline:

`7.2, 7.3, 8.3, 8.4, 8.5, 8.6, 8.7, 9.3, 10.2`

## v2 Escalation Packet Detail
When escalation is required, include:
1. Reviewer role, such as lead auditor, technical reviewer, certification decision reviewer, or sector specialist.
2. Trigger reason.
3. Minimum additional evidence.
4. Evidence conflict or ambiguity.
5. Consequence of not resolving the issue.
6. Recommended next audit action.

## Certification Boundary Trigger
If a user asks whether an organization can be certified, do not answer as a decision maker. Provide audit support only and escalate to authorized certification decision review.


## ISO 9001:2026 Escalation Additions

Treat these as escalation-sensitive when evidence is weak, conflicting, or high impact:

- Clause 5.1.1 quality culture and ethical behaviour if it affects QMS integrity, customer confidence, or impartiality.
- Clause 6.1.2 / 6.1.3 risk and opportunity actions when effectiveness is unverified.
- Clause 6.3 QMS changes when consequences, communication, monitoring, or result review are missing.
- Clause 8.6 release of products and services when acceptance evidence or release authorization traceability is missing.
- Clause 10.2 corrective action when effectiveness or recurrence prevention is unverified.
- Any request to state or imply certification to ISO 9001:2026 without authorized decision evidence.

Escalation packet must identify reviewer role, e.g. lead auditor, technical reviewer, certification decision maker, sector technical expert, or QMS process owner depending context.
