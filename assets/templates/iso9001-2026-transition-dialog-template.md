# ISO 9001:2026 Transition Dialog Template

## Opening
I will review transition readiness against ISO 9001:2026 themes and objective evidence. I will not treat the draft assessment as a certification decision.

## Transition questions
1. How has the organization evaluated climate change relevance in context and interested-party requirements?
2. How do leadership and awareness processes address quality culture and ethical behaviour?
3. How are risks and opportunities determined, analysed, evaluated, acted on, and reviewed separately?
4. How are QMS changes planned, communicated, monitored, evaluated, and reviewed for effectiveness?
5. What organizational knowledge must be retained, applied, shared, and updated?
6. How is documented evidence protected from unintended alteration or deletion?

## Evidence to request
- Context/interested party review
- Risk and opportunity records
- Change plans and post-change reviews
- Awareness/communication evidence
- Knowledge retention and sharing evidence
- Management review records
- Internal audit transition gap results
